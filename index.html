WELCOME TO KOMAPE ATTORNEYS

Accessible legal support. Thoughtful representation.

Legal help that respects your circumstances.

Komape Attorneys is a community-focused practice established in 2022, based at 99 Julius Nyerere Street in Tembisa's Ibazelo Section.

Led by founder and director Godfrey Komape, the practice assists families, individuals and businesses with litigation, criminal matters, divorce, property disputes, debt collection, deceased estates and related legal services.

Practical, clear legal guidance — with attention to the people behind every matter.
