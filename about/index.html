OUR FIRM

Representation built around your needs.

Founded in 2022 by Godfrey Komape, Komape Attorneys is a community-driven practice in the Ibazelo Section of Tembisa, Gauteng.

The firm's published profile highlights legal assistance for families, individuals and businesses in and around Tembisa and nearby areas, including Ivory Park, Phomolong, Winnie Mandela, Makulong and Sedibeng.

Its services include divorce and family disputes, bail and criminal defence, civil and commercial litigation, collections, evictions, deceased estates and correspondent work.
