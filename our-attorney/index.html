FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Godfrey Komape

Attorney · Komape Attorneys

Godfrey Komape is the founding director of Komape Attorneys, a Tembisa-based practice established in 2022.

The firm's public directory describes a community-focused approach, providing accessible legal support to individuals, families and businesses in Tembisa and surrounding areas.

Its published services span family law and divorce, criminal representation, civil litigation, debt recovery, property and evictions, wills and estate administration, as well as correspondent services.

Clients should confirm professional details, service scope and appointment availability directly with the office.
